# Declaration on Historical Integrity and the Hermeneutics of the Biblical Writings
The Manifesto of the Approbatio Testimonii Historica (APTH) proposes a hermeneutical framework designed to overcome the long standing impasse in biblical interpretation. It systematically addresses the limitations of three dominant approaches: absolute inerrancy, liberal theology, and anti intellectualism. Additionally, it critiques dialectical, existential, and canonical narrative approaches for falling into epistemological circularity by severing historical verifiability from meaningful testimony.  

Rooted in critical realism, the APTH resolves these failures by distinguishing the historical facticity of divine action from the ancient literary format used to communicate it. Grounded in four core axioms, the methodology establishes a personal creator God who leaves graspable historical traces within the 66 book Protestant canon, transmitted via authentic human authors.  Methodologically, the APTH integrates Speech Act Theory to discern original authorial intention and utilizes inference to the best explanation to evaluate transcendent interventions based on historical explanatory power rather than gap filling. 

By employing full historical critical tools while rejecting methodological naturalism, the framework introduces monotheistically augmented historical methods, an approach that openly admits divine action as a plausible hypothesis where immanent explanations prove insufficient. Operating under the research paradigm of the Messianic Logic of Fulfillment, the APTH upholds rigorous scientific investigation and intellectual virtues like epistemic humility and honesty without sacrificing the core historical substance of Christian revelation.
